## Description (≤4000 chars)
```
Hummingbird is the honest way to test your own read on stocks and crypto.

CALL IT BEFORE YOU PEEK
Predict Higher or Lower — and how sure you are — before you see the sketch. Hummingbird keeps an honest score of your own calls over time, and shows you something no hype app will: whether feeling "Confident" actually meant being right.

SEE IF YOU BEAT THE METHODS
Once your calls resolve against real prices, see how you did — and whether your own calls are beating the app's simple forecasting methods on the very same calls.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• Educational sketches from public prices — never buy/sell signals, never advice.
• An accuracy report that shows how wrong the app has been. Receipts, not promises.
• A reliability score for every sketch, with the math shown.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything runs on your iPhone. No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no data collected.
• Your call record and watchlist stay on your device.

ALSO INSIDE
• Simple, transparent methods (Drift, Trend, Holt, and more) to compare side by side.
• A private watchlist, Home & Lock Screen widgets, a once-a-day morning read, and an optional Live Activity.

Free to use. Optional Pro goes deeper on your own record — You vs. the methods, your full call history, and calibration by confidence — plus a pay-once Lifetime option. Never better foresight; never advice.

Hummingbird is an educational tool. It draws simple projections from public price history — not predictions, price targets, or financial advice. Markets are unpredictable.
```

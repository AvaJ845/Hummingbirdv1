# Content templates — the honest-scoreboard engine

The product generates these. Fill the brackets from the app's own data (Settings
→ Accuracy report) and your own "Your calls" record. Keep every claim a record
of the past — never advice, never a promise.

---

## 1. "How wrong were we?" (recurring — the flagship)
Cadence: once per resolution cycle (weekly to start, then monthly). Source: the
app's Accuracy Report.

**Title:** How wrong was Hummingbird this [week/month]?

We build an app that refuses to sell you certainty — so here's ours, in public.

This [period], across [N] sketches that resolved against real prices:
- **Typical miss:** [X]% — how far a sketch usually landed from the real price
- **Ranges held:** [Y]% of the time (we aim for ~80%)
- **Best-tracking method:** [method] on [asset]

[1–2 honest sentences: where it did well, where it whiffed, what conditions hurt.]

No signals. No advice. Just a record of the past — including the parts we got wrong.
→ [App Store link] · [attach the shareable accuracy card]

**Where:** blog/SEO, then a condensed thread on X, then r/investing / r/CryptoCurrency as *value* (not a plug).

- **[ASSET]** — …

## 3. Per-asset method post (SEO drip — evergreen)
Cadence: a steady trickle (1–2/week). Source: per-asset scorecard.

**Title:** Which simple method tracked [ASSET] best last month?

A plain-English, no-hype look: over the last [N] days, [method A] typically
missed [ASSET] by [X]%, [method B] by [Y]%. Here's what that does and doesn't
mean — and why "best lately" is a record, not a promise.

[Short honest body + the not-advice line.]

**Where:** blog (ranks for long-tail "[asset] price method / trend" searches).

---

## 4. Anti-hype essay (positioning — occasional)
Ideas, each a standalone piece:
- "Confidence isn't accuracy: the calibration trap every investor falls into."
- "Why we refuse to give buy/sell signals (and why that's the point)."
- "What a price 'sketch' is — and what it definitely isn't."
- "We publish our own error rate. No finance app does that. Here's why we do."

**Where:** blog + Hacker News (the honesty/privacy angle is HN-shaped) + Product Hunt launch narrative.

---

## Launch spikes (do once, when polished + ~2 weeks of real data)
- **Show HN:** "Hummingbird – an on-device finance app that publishes its own error rate and refuses to give signals." Lead with privacy + honesty + technical transparency.
- **Product Hunt:** the honest-mirror angle; use pillar #2 (your diary) as the maker story.

## Measure
Install source · App Store impressions→installs · **calls per new user** (activation) ·
**Day-7 / Day-30 return** (the retention test the whole pivot exists for) · Pro conversion.

Not another prediction app

Most market apps sell certainty they don't have. Hummingbird does the opposite — it draws simple, transparent “sketches” from public prices, scores how reliable each one is, publishes how wrong past sketches have been, and stays honest about the limits.

🔒

On-device & private

Everything runs on your iPhone. No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no data collected.

✎

Honest, not hype

Simple methods sketch a possible path from public prices — clearly labeled educational, never buy/sell advice.

◎

Reliability, scored

Every sketch gets a 0–100 read from how well it's tracked before, current conditions, and how far ahead it reaches — with the math shown.

✓

Shows its work — misses and all

An accuracy report tracks how close past sketches actually landed once real prices caught up — and which method has tracked each asset best. A record of the past, not a promise.

🎯

Optional: check your own calibration

The honesty pledge

Hummingbird produces educational projections from public prices — not predictions, price targets, or financial advice. Markets are unpredictable. It will never tell you to buy or sell, and it never sees an account, a balance, or a broker.

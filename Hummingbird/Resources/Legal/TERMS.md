# Hummingbird Terms of Use

**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

## Educational use only — not financial advice

Hummingbird provides **on-device statistical sketches** from public historical prices and simple models. It is for learning and exploration only.

Hummingbird is **not** financial, investment, tax, or trading advice. It does **not** provide price targets, signals, or guarantees. Markets are unpredictable. Do not make money decisions based solely on this app.

## How projections work

You choose a symbol and horizon. The app fetches public history when available (or uses clearly labeled sample data offline) and runs simple models on your device. Different methods can show different paths. Results are **untested** educational sketches — not PhD-certified forecasts and not a claim of accuracy.

## Hummingbird Pro
